# UNDERREALM loop

Play Gold and Cinder only. NEAR settlement is not wired. Nothing in a raid spends real money.

## Every 30 seconds

One trap action. One hero response you can see. One page decision when a hero dies. One Gold, stock, or price change.

## Every 5 minutes

A few waves. A burned-versus-landed tally. Salvage spent or supplied. One merchant sale. One hero purchase with a reason. A shop or price consequence.

## Every session

Vault and results. Materials used. Hero memories. Merchant status. A replay of the recorded ticks. An optional listing. No deed, guild, or withdrawal screen in this build.
