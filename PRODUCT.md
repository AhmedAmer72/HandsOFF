# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

A guest on this device keeps a dungeon. They are the owner, not a hero. Confirmed by the v10.2 spec the user attached and by the running game.

## Product Purpose

UNDERREALM is a dungeon-defense game. The player builds traps, defends the vault, and decides whether a fallen hero’s diary page is burned or becomes a rumour. Success is a hall that stays open and a rumour economy that changes the next raid.

## Positioning

The player owns the dungeon. Kara, Brann, and Ivo are the only raiders. Patrons can fund. Spectators watch. The player does not invade another hall.

## Operating Context

The website opens on a landing page. Get Started enters My Dungeon as a guest. In the game the places are My Dungeon, World Map, Market, and Workshop. Market and workshop are physical rooms. E talks when the player is close.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Permits, settlement, deeds, and merchant NEAR actions run on NEAR testnet. Play gold and Cinder stay in the hall. The dev server signs with the near-cli keychain. No wallet prompt is part of the raid walk. The world map lists halls published in this browser. Economy values in `rules/economy.ts` stay as written. Recipes stay spike, pit, boulder, potion, armor, and boots. Mira, Orrin, and Tallow are the only merchants.

NEAR AI, OutLayer, Intents, NEAR Auth, and the DAO are not connected. Rumours are not minted until a verifier key is pinned.

## Brand Commitments

Name: UNDERREALM. Tagline: “Your dungeon. Their money. Your rules.” The user pinned a dark hall: slate stone, torchlight, bronze and gold, parchment, fog, and the KayKit models already in the project. Gold is the accent. The headline on the entrance is “You own the dungeon. The heroes are your customers.”

## Evidence on Hand

The playable hall, market, workshop, and device-local map are in this repository. KayKit dungeon, weapon, and hex models are the pictures. There are no press quotes, live player counts, or mainnet receipts. Do not invent them.

## Product Principles

- The player defends. Heroes raid.
- A diary page is a decision, not a loot popup.
- Vault gold and anything that could leave the device stay separate.
- Say when a thing is only on this device.
- The entrance explains the game. Get Started is how you enter it.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Controls stay at least 44px. Motion respects `prefers-reduced-motion`. Focus rings stay visible. No product-specific standard beyond that was set.
